# Reviewer walkthrough

Source: https://github.com/ankushkhakale/OAuth-Atlas

Follow the [setup instructions](../../README.md#get-running), then open the app. No sign-in is needed.

## A quick lap

1. **Browse the directory.** Filter the Indian-platform collection and pin an app. Entries are research starting points, not verified integrations.
2. **Open Gmail.** Switch read/modify to send-only. Look at the scope guidance, separate effort/wait labels, and linked sources. Tick a step.
3. **Compare Slack and GitHub.** Their distribution and workspace/organization gates differ.
4. **Run live research.** Try `https://linear.app/developers/oauth-2-0-authentication`. Follow the progress, then inspect coverage and sources.
5. **Try an unfamiliar name.** Confirm a suggested identity when available, or add its homepage. An incomplete report should explain the missing evidence without claiming OAuth is unsupported.
6. **Check persistence and sharing.** Open My guides, reload, then copy/export a report. Reports stay in this browser; Markdown is the portable format.
7. **Open Evidence & method.** The linked Composio directory explains managed OAuth application ownership separately from each user's consent.

## What's included

A searchable directory, four reviewed guides, live documentation research, comparison, source inspection, setup checklists, browser-local saving, and Markdown copy/export. No account system or database.

Atlas researches documentation. It does not register provider apps, exchange tokens, or submit reviews. Undocumented requirements and exact approval times remain unconfirmed. The [validation record](validation.md) separates executed checks from supplied instructions and known gaps.

This walkthrough covers the demonstrated product scope. It does not certify a match against an unavailable assignment rubric, and it is not a substitute for a requested recording. No submission form or message was sent to the assignment owner.
